---

## Before you spend a penny — fix the price claim

Your booking engine charges **£45 for one room** with a **£90 minimum charge**, and the bundle is
**"Any 3 areas for £99"**. But the service pages say *"Starting from £39"* and the area pages say
*"from £39 for a single room"*, and the old campaign doc and `/4for99` page advertise
**4 carpets for £99**.

Three different prices for the same job. That matters here because:

- Google checks that a price in the ad is actually achievable on the landing page. A "£39" ad
  pointing at a page that quotes £90 minimum risks a **Misrepresentation** disapproval.
- Customers who click a £39 ad and get quoted £90 bounce, and you have paid for the click.

Pick one number and make the site, the ads and the booking engine agree before launch.
`Any 3 areas for £99` is the one the engine actually honours, so that is the safe claim.

## Start here, don't paste all of it on day one

There are ~950 keywords below. Splitting £20/day across every ad group means none of them
get enough impressions to learn. Launch **Phase 1** only, then add a group a week.

| **4 — only if you have spare budget** | The village/suburb groups · Commercial · Hard Floors | Long tail; most will show "Low search volume" |
